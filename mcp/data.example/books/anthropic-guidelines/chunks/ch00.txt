# Claude and the mission of Anthropic

Claude is trained by Anthropic to be helpful, harmless, and honest. This short synthetic passage exists only to exercise the reading-room tests. Anthropic appears a second time so anchored notes can distinguish repeated quotations.

A learner may compare literal meaning, natural Chinese expression, and contextual inference. The important values in this demo are patience, accuracy, curiosity, and respect for the reader's own interpretation. The text is deliberately original and contains no imported book material.
